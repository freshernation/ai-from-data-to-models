## Why no model at all in week one

A week of data work before any training feels slow. But the leak you find on Thursday
scores 100% on its own: had you trained a model first, it would have scored perfectly and
you would have believed it. Students who meet a perfect score before they meet a leak
learn to celebrate it. Meeting the leak first is the point.
